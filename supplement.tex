\subsection*{S5a. Exploratory time-profile and culture-deletion sensitivity (new)}
For GSE152250 only, the nine possible leave-one-culture-out-from-each-arm comparisons were calculated separately at 6, 24 and 48 h, each using the log$_2$ ratio of mean native FPKM among the remaining two cultures per arm. These are deterministic influence checks, not bootstrap confidence intervals or independent biological replication. The two-sided exploratory 6-to-24-h treatment-by-time statistic uses difference of the respective treatment-minus-control arm means of log$_2$(FPKM$+0.5$); this transformed-mean contrast must not be mistaken for the log$_2$ ratio of mean FPKM in Table~\ref{tab:gse152250}. At each time point, label exchangeability under the null yields $\binom{6}{3}=20$ allocations; the interaction uses all $20\times20=400$ pairwise timepoint allocations. Its unadjusted two-sided reference is the fraction with absolute contrast at least as large as observed. Because it was examined after the temporal pattern was available and original randomization is not demonstrated here, this is a descriptive, conditional exact permutation sensitivity, not a confirmatory randomized-treatment interaction test.

\section*{S6. Spatial and day-scale analyses}
For the predefined GSE265828 TGFB1--TGFBR1--TGFBR2 axis, Moran's $I$ was 0.24, 0.17, and 0.26 in the three post-infarction sections ($q=0.013$ each) and $-0.02$ in control ($q=0.895$). The inference is spatial organization of a transcript covariate, not physical ligand diffusion. The permutation design and multiplicity control are specified in the main Methods; this result is not used to support a stronger physical claim. Figure~\ref{fig:s3spatial} shows the section-level Moran summaries.

In GSE267256, day explained 0.973 of Cthrc1 variability and 0.916 of the equal-$z$ repair-module score. Because day 5 has one sample, the analysis uses a 3--5-day reparative window rather than a precise day-5 peak. Excluding day 5, days 3--4 minus days 1--2 gave +3.03 log$_2$CPM for Cthrc1 ($q=0.00262$).

The 2026 multimodal post-MI study further reports a 3--5 dpi transition window for Cthrc1$^+$ reparative cardiac fibroblasts, single-cell trajectories toward reparative states, and spatial enrichment progressing from remote/border regions toward the infarct zone \cite{Hernandez2026}. RNAscope provides a local transition from Postn$^+$, Aspn$^+$, Cthrc1$^-$ toward Postn$^+$, Aspn$^-$, Cthrc1$^+$ fibroblasts. These published observations are used as state-transition and anatomical-context evidence, not as a diffusion estimate or spontaneous Turing-pattern test.

\section*{S7. Sub-day timing and the hard-delay boundary}
In GSE123018, the earliest sampled 4/4 donor concordance was 2 h for RNA and 6 h for ribosome profiling. These are sampling landmarks, not onset times and not a myocardial delay.

The fixed-delay diagnostic summary reports an optimum $\delta=0.116$ h, an originally calculated profile spanning 0--2.1 h, and an AICc 2.82 units above the best candidate. Here $\delta$ is a response-diagnostic quantity and is distinct from the mechanistic delay $\tau$. Zero remains admissible, and the original four-family model-specification file is unavailable. The full interpretation and boundary-aware statistical rationale are therefore reported in the main Methods. The conclusion supported by these data is limited to: current data do not establish a strictly positive hard delay.

\section*{S10. Candidate-state topology admissibility}
The 2026 mouse post-MI source provides an observationally resolved local transition from Postn$^+$, Aspn$^+$, Cthrc1$^-$ activated fibroblasts toward Postn$^+$, Aspn$^-$, Cthrc1$^+$ reparative fibroblasts \cite{Hernandez2026}. This supports ASPN as an observational state-transition candidate. It does not establish that ASPN is consumed by the same reaction that produces CTHRC1, nor does it identify a rate law of the form $u^2v$ under an explicit mapping of CTHRC1- and ASPN-associated states to $u$ and $v$.

Wang et al. report that recombinant WNT5A can partially rescue Cthrc1-deficient post-infarction repair phenotypes and place CTHRC1 within non-canonical WNT5A signaling \cite{Wang2023}. This makes WNT5A a mechanistic pathway candidate, but not an opposite-state readout. The roles are therefore complementary rather than interchangeable. GSE123018 RNA and Ribo remain two assays of the same target and are not treated as two independent dynamical states. Table~\ref{tab:stateaudit} summarizes the distinct evidence roles and the missing requirements for direct Schnakenberg-state assignment.

\section*{S11. Cross-context application}
The cross-context transfer criteria are defined in the main Methods. In the Saudi-center application, the 24-h fibroblast result cannot be interpreted as evidence that \cthrc{} is increased in whole end-stage DCM myocardium without a cell-state/tissue bridge. Conversely, whole-left-ventricular DCM tissue cannot determine fibroblast-specific \tgfb{} causality. The application therefore retains each study-specific result without cross-study magnitude pooling and identifies the cell-resolved measurement needed for a stronger inference.
